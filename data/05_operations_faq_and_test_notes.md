## 1. Admissions workflow
1. Review the catalogue and choose a course. 2. Complete the application. 3. Admissions checks eligibility and cohort capacity. 4. Pay the application fee and follow the current tuition deadline. 5. Admissions shares confirmation and the timetable if a place is approved.

The assistant cannot submit applications, approve students, accept payments, or reserve seats. If no cohort notice gives a start date, it must abstain.

## 2. Class and learning-resource support
For a missing class link or resource, check the cohort platform and contact student support with the course, cohort, and issue. Never send passwords or one-time passcodes. Private class links and instructor contacts are not in this knowledge base.

For a campus closure, students should follow the official cohort notice. The current policy allows the institution to move an affected session online or announce a replacement date. Students should not assume that every campus closure cancels the entire course.

## 3. Certificates
A certificate requires the current attendance threshold and the course's completion requirements. No universal grading scale or individual academic records are provided; the assistant cannot confirm a student's result or eligibility.

## 4. Frequently asked questions
**Q: Is the application fee included in the course tuition?**
A: No. The application fee is separate; see the current policy for its amount.

**Q: Can I get a full refund if I withdraw whenever I want?**
A: No. The standard refund depends on notice timing; the current policy has an institution-cancellation exception.

**Q: Does every course run in Hyderabad?**
A: No. Location depends on the course and announced cohort.

**Q: Does completing Career Readiness guarantee a job?**
A: No. It provides preparation, not a job or internship guarantee.

**Q: What are the help desk hours?**
A: Monday–Saturday, 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. Pakistan Standard Time, excluding announced public holidays.

**Q: What is the exact date of the next AI and Data Foundations cohort?**
A: This knowledge base does not contain a next-cohort start date. The assistant must abstain and refer the user to admissions.

## 5. Deliberately undocumented information
The percentage discount, if any, offered to siblings enrolling in the same term is deliberately not specified in any Meridian University document. No sibling discount percentage, eligibility rule, or application process is documented. The correct assistant response to a question asking for this percentage is: "I could not find enough information in the provided documents." Do not estimate a discount or invent a special offer.

Future cohort start dates require a cohort-specific notice.

## 8. Index refresh and evidence quality
