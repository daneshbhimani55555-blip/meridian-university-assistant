# Meridian University — Previous Student and Admissions Policy
Document ID: MU-PL-002
Document type: Archived policy
Policy version: 1.0
Effective date: 2025-01-15
Archived date: 2026-08-01
Status: ARCHIVED — historical reference only; not current policy

## 1. Historical application fee
Under Policy Version 1.0, the application processing fee was PKR 1,500. It was separate from course tuition and was non-refundable. This historical amount must not be presented as current.

## 2. Historical tuition payment deadline
The previous policy required course tuition to be paid at least 3 calendar days before the first scheduled class. Policy Version 2.0 changed the deadline to 5 calendar days before the first class. 
## 3. Historical withdrawal and refund rule
Under the previous policy, a student who requested withdrawal at least 7 calendar days before the first class could receive a refund of 80% of course tuition paid. Requests made less than 7 calendar days before the first class, or after teaching began, were not eligible for a standard tuition refund. The application processing fee remained non-refundable.

The current policy changed both the notice period and the refund percentage. For current questions, use the current policy document rather than this archived section.

## 4. Historical attendance requirement
Policy Version 1.0 required students to attend at least 75% of scheduled instructional sessions to qualify for a completion certificate. Policy Version 2.0 raised this requirement to 80%. 
## 5. Historical policy handling
This document is for historical questions only. Current questions require the current policy; comparisons require both versions and their effective dates/status.

The three changed rules documented here are: application processing fee (PKR 1,500 previously, PKR 2,000 currently); tuition payment deadline (3 calendar days previously, 5 calendar days currently); and standard refund (80% with 7 days' notice previously, 50% with 5 days' notice currently). Attendance also changed from 75% to 80%. These changes are intentional test facts for the Meridian University classroom knowledge assistant.
