## 1. Meridian University at a glance
Meridian University is a fictional skills-focused learning institution. It runs short, practical courses of 4 to 16 weeks. It has two fictional learning locations: Meridian Learning Campus, Karachi (administrative hub, main labs) and Meridian Learning Campus, Hyderabad (selected weekend classes). Many courses also run online. Typical cohort size is 20 to 35 students so that instructors can give individual feedback. The institution teaches practical skills; it is not a degree-awarding body, and course completion certificates are not academic degrees.

## 2. Intakes and cohort start dates
Meridian opens new cohorts several times a year, but the exact start date of any cohort is published only in a cohort-specific notice from admissions. This document does not list any cohort start dates. If a user asks for the date of the next cohort of any course, the assistant must say that it could not find enough information in the provided documents and refer the user to admissions@meridian-university.example.

## 3. Usual class timings
Class timings are announced in each cohort notice. The usual patterns are: weekday evening batches (about 6:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m.), weekend morning batches (about 10:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.), and online live sessions in the evening. A typical weekday batch meets 3 times per week; a typical weekend batch meets once or twice per weekend. These are usual patterns only, and the cohort notice is the final authority for the exact days and times.

## 4. Course details — AI and Data Foundations
Duration 12 weeks; tuition PKR 18,000. Prerequisite: basic computer use; no programming background needed. Weekly flow: weeks 1–3 data basics and spreadsheet cleaning; weeks 4–6 charts, summaries and data storytelling; weeks 7–9 responsible use of AI tools, prompt writing and checking AI output for errors; weeks 10–12 introduction to basic machine-learning ideas and the final data interpretation project. Tools used: spreadsheet software and browser-based AI tools. Assessment: weekly practice tasks and the small final project. Delivery: Karachi campus or online, subject to the cohort listing.

## 5. Course details — Web Development
Duration 16 weeks; tuition PKR 22,000. Prerequisite: basic computer use and comfort with typing. Weekly flow: weeks 1–4 HTML and page structure; weeks 5–8 CSS, layout, and responsive design; weeks 9–13 JavaScript fundamentals and page interactivity; weeks 14–16 the guided website project, testing, and presentation. Tools used: a code editor, a web browser with developer tools, and a free version-control account. Assessment: weekly build tasks and the final responsive website project. Delivery: Karachi or online, subject to the cohort listing.

## 6. Course details — Cybersecurity Basics
Duration 10 weeks; tuition PKR 16,500. Prerequisite: basic computer and internet use. It is an introductory, defensive-awareness course. Weekly flow: weeks 1–2 security concepts and common threats; weeks 3–4 password hygiene, multi-factor authentication and account safety; weeks 5–6 safe browsing, phishing awareness and device safety; weeks 7–8 privacy awareness and safe data handling; weeks 9–10 a personal security checklist project. The course does not teach attacking systems. Delivery: Karachi campus.

## 7. Course details — Graphic Design Essentials
Duration 8 weeks; tuition PKR 14,000. Prerequisite: basic computer use; no drawing skill is required. Weekly flow: weeks 1–2 visual hierarchy and layout; weeks 3–4 typography; weeks 5–6 color principles and image preparation; weeks 7–8 the portfolio task, where each student creates a small set of digital design assets such as a poster, a social media post and a simple logo layout. Delivery: Karachi, or Hyderabad when announced.

## 8. Course details — English Communication
Duration 8 weeks; tuition PKR 9,500. Prerequisite: basic reading ability in English. Weekly flow: weeks 1–2 everyday speaking and listening; weeks 3–4 vocabulary for study and work; weeks 5–6 writing clear emails and short messages; weeks 7–8 short presentations with feedback. Students practice in pairs and small groups. Delivery: Karachi, Hyderabad weekend cohort, or online when listed.

## 9. Course details — Office Productivity
Duration 6 weeks; tuition PKR 7,500. Prerequisite: none beyond basic computer use. Weekly flow: week 1 file organization and document formatting; weeks 2–3 spreadsheets, formulas and simple charts; week 4 presentations; weeks 5–6 collaborative work habits and a small practice assignment. Practice uses sample data only, never real personal or business records. Delivery: Karachi or online.

## 10. Course details — Digital Marketing
Duration 8 weeks; tuition PKR 12,500. Prerequisite: basic internet and social media use. Weekly flow: weeks 1–2 audience research; weeks 3–4 content planning and calendars; weeks 5–6 search fundamentals; weeks 7–8 measuring a campaign and ethical communication. The course does not provide advertising credits and does not promise customer leads, sales, or followers. Delivery: Karachi or online.

## 11. Course details — Career Readiness
Duration 4 weeks; tuition PKR 5,000. Prerequisite: none. Weekly flow: week 1 CV structure; week 2 interview preparation and practice; week 3 professional communication and teamwork; week 4 a job-search plan. Students may practice mock interviews with classmates. The course provides learning support only; it does not guarantee an internship, interview, or job offer. Delivery: online or selected campus workshops.

## 12. Who can apply
Applicants are generally expected to be at least 16 years old and able to follow instruction in English or the language stated in the course listing. Admissions confirms individual eligibility and whether a seat is available in the cohort. Applicants under 18 may be asked for a parent or guardian's contact details. Holding a university degree is not required for any listed course. Applying does not guarantee admission.

## 13. Documents commonly requested at application
Admissions may ask for: a completed application form, a copy of the applicant's identity document or student form, a recent passport-size photograph, details of the last completed education, and a working phone number and email address. Applicants should never send passwords, one-time passcodes, or banking logins. The assistant cannot receive or check documents; only the admissions office can.

## 14. Payment channels (fictional)
For this classroom project, the institution is described as accepting payments through bank transfer, a mobile wallet transfer, or cash at the Karachi campus cashier counter during help-desk hours. The amounts and deadlines are in the catalogue and in the current policy. Students should keep the receipt and the transaction reference. The assistant cannot confirm that a payment has been received.

## 15. Karachi campus facilities
The Karachi campus is the administrative hub. Facilities described for this project: two computer labs (about 30 seats each), three classrooms, a small resource and reading room, a student help desk, a cashier counter, a quiet study corner, drinking water points, a prayer room, and a small cafeteria. Wi-Fi is available for enrolled students in learning areas. Specific room numbers and floor maps are not documented in this knowledge base.

## 16. Hyderabad campus facilities
The Hyderabad campus is smaller and is used for selected weekend classes. Facilities described for this project: two classrooms, one small computer lab, a reception desk, and a prayer area. Not every course runs in Hyderabad. The course listing and cohort notice state the location. Cashier services and the full help desk are based in Karachi.

## 17. Accessibility and special needs
Meridian University aims to make learning spaces usable for all students. Students who need a reasonable adjustment, such as seating near the front, large-print material, or step-free access to a classroom, should write to students@meridian-university.example before the course starts, with the course name and the cohort. Student Support will review the request. Approval of a specific adjustment is not guaranteed in this document, and no medical details should be sent by email unless Student Support asks for them.

## 18. Learning platform — Meridian Learn
Meridian Learn is the fictional learning platform used for course materials. After enrolment is confirmed, students receive access instructions from admissions or Student Support. Instructors post lesson notes, practice files, assignment instructions, and announcements on the platform. Online cohorts use it to share the live session link. If a class link or resource is missing, check the platform first, then contact students@meridian-university.example with the course, cohort, and issue.

## 19. Recordings and materials
Some online live sessions may be recorded for students of that cohort, but recordings are not promised for every class. Recordings and course materials are for the enrolled student's own learning. Students must not share course materials, login details, or recordings publicly or sell them. Materials remain available for the duration stated in the cohort notice.

## 20. Assignments and projects
Most courses use small weekly practice tasks and one final project or portfolio task. Instructions and deadlines for each task are posted on the learning platform. Late work is handled by the instructor under the cohort's instructions. This knowledge base does not provide a universal grading scale, pass mark, or individual results, and the assistant cannot confirm any student's marks or result.

## 21. Academic honesty and use of AI tools
Students must submit their own work. Copying another student's work, or presenting copied material as one's own, is academic dishonesty. AI tools may be used for learning and practice when the instructor allows it, but a student must understand, check, and be able to explain any work they submit. Where a course task says that AI tools are not allowed, they must not be used for that task. Meridian does not publish a fixed penalty table in this knowledge base; the instructor refers serious cases to Student Support.

## 22. Student conduct
Students are expected to be respectful toward classmates, instructors, and staff. Harassment, discrimination, threats, damaging equipment, or misusing the institution's network are not acceptable. Mobile phones should be silent during class. Students should not install unapproved software on lab computers. Concerns about conduct can be reported in writing to students@meridian-university.example.

## 23. Certificates — more detail
A completion certificate is awarded to a student who meets the current attendance requirement (at least 80% of scheduled instructional sessions) and the course's completion requirements. The certificate states the student's name, the course name, and the completion period. For this project, certificates are described as digital PDF files shared by the institution after completion is confirmed. A certificate is proof of course completion only; it is not a degree. Certificates are not issued for incomplete or unpaid enrolments. The assistant cannot check or issue a certificate.

## 24. Student support — what it can and cannot do
Student Support can help with: missing course materials, platform access problems, timetable questions, requests for reasonable adjustments, documented-emergency attendance requests (within 3 calendar days after returning, under the current policy), and complaints. Student Support cannot: share another student's information, promise private tutoring, change a published fee, approve an exception that the policy does not allow, or guarantee a job or internship. Email: students@meridian-university.example.

## 25. Events and learning activities
Meridian may arrange activities for enrolled students such as an orientation session before classes begin, hands-on workshops, short guest talks, a project showcase at the end of a cohort, and mock-interview sessions for Career Readiness students. Whether an activity happens in a particular cohort is stated in that cohort's notice. Attendance at optional activities does not replace the 80% attendance requirement for instructional sessions.

## 26. Privacy and data handling
The institution collects only the information needed for admission, communication, attendance, and certificates. Students should not post their personal details in public forums. This assistant does not store or see student records, grades, personal phone numbers, or private account information, and users should not type such details into it. In a real deployment, the institution would need approved privacy controls and access permissions.

For general questions about the institution: help@meridian-university.example. For applications, eligibility, cohort capacity, payments arrangements, and start dates: admissions@meridian-university.example. For enrolled-student issues, complaints, adjustments, and emergency attendance requests: students@meridian-university.example. ## 31. Glossary
Application processing fee: the separate, non-refundable fee paid when applying; the current amount is in the current policy. Tuition: the course fee listed in the catalogue. Cohort: a group of students who take a course together in one run. Cohort notice: the official message that states a cohort's dates, timings, location, and delivery mode. Instructional session: a scheduled class counted for attendance. Completion certificate: the document awarded after meeting the attendance and completion requirements. Archived policy: an old policy kept only for historical comparison.
