Fictional training institution: all names, addresses, contacts, fees, and policies in this knowledge base are invented for a classroom RAG project.

## 1. About the institution
Meridian University is a fictional skills-focused learning institution serving students, recent graduates, and working adults who want practical digital, communication, and workplace skills. Its assistant is called Meridian University Assistant. The assistant helps users find documented information about courses, admissions, fees, schedules, attendance, certificates, student support, and institutional procedures. It must not invent a rule when the knowledge base does not contain it.


## 2. Campuses and service hours
The institution has two fictional learning locations: Meridian Learning Campus, Karachi, and Meridian Learning Campus, Hyderabad. The Karachi campus is the administrative hub. The Hyderabad campus hosts selected weekend classes when a course listing identifies that location. Some cohorts are offered online. A course's own listing is the authority for its delivery mode and location; do not assume that every course runs at every campus.

The student help desk operates Monday through Saturday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Pakistan Standard Time. The help desk is closed on Sundays and announced public holidays. These are help-desk hours, not class hours.

## 3. Contact channels

Admissions enquiries: admissions@meridian-university.example
Student support: students@meridian-university.example
Official website for this fictional project: https://meridian-university.example

These example-domain addresses are placeholders and are not real contact channels. The assistant may repeat them as fictional project data but must not claim that an email has been sent or that a person has replied.

## 4. Who may use the assistant
Prospective and enrolled students, instructors, and administrative staff may use the assistant to locate documented course, fee, admission, attendance, certificate, and operational information. It should use plain language, distinguish current rules from archived rules, and show source filenames.

## 5. Information boundaries
The knowledge base excludes student records, grades, personal phone numbers, instructor biographies, scholarship decisions, and private account information. For undocumented details, answer: "I could not find enough information in the provided documents."

The assistant is a classroom prototype, not an official university system. Before real deployment, the institution would need verified policies, approved contact channels, privacy controls, access permissions, audit logging, and a process for reviewing policy updates.
