# Meridian University — Current Student and Admissions Policy
Document ID: MU-PL-003
Document type: Policy
Policy version: 2.0
Effective date: 2026-08-01
Status: CURRENT — use for questions about rules that apply now

## 1. Applications and admission
Applicants complete the official application form, provide the requested contact and education details, select a listed course, and pay the non-refundable application processing fee of PKR 2,000. The application fee is separate from course tuition. Applying does not guarantee admission or a seat; admissions confirms eligibility and cohort capacity.

Applicants should submit an application at least 5 calendar days before the advertised course start date. Late applications may be considered only if admissions confirms that a place is available. The knowledge base does not promise that late applications will be accepted.

## 2. Tuition payment
Course tuition must be paid no later than 5 calendar days before the first scheduled class, unless the admissions office has issued a written alternative arrangement for that student. The published course catalogue lists tuition amounts. The application processing fee is additional and is not deducted from tuition.

Keep the receipt and transaction reference for payment enquiries. Never provide passwords, one-time passcodes, full card numbers, or banking logins. The assistant cannot verify payments.

## 3. Withdrawal and refunds
A student who requests withdrawal at least 5 calendar days before the first class may receive a refund of 50% of the course tuition paid. The application processing fee is non-refundable. A withdrawal request received fewer than 5 calendar days before the first class, or after classes have started, is not eligible for a tuition refund under the standard rule.

**Exception 1 — institution cancellation:** If Meridian University cancels a cohort and cannot offer a reasonable replacement schedule, the student may choose a full refund of course tuition or transfer to another available cohort. This exception applies because the institution, rather than the student, cancels the course. The application processing fee is also returned when the institution cancels the cohort before teaching begins.

## 4. Attendance and certificate eligibility
Students must attend at least 80% of scheduled instructional sessions to qualify for a course completion certificate. Attendance is recorded by the instructor or the approved online learning platform. **Exception 2 — documented emergency:** A student who misses sessions because of a serious unexpected emergency may submit a written request with supporting documentation to Student Support within 3 calendar days after returning to classes. Student Support may approve a make-up learning plan. Approval is not automatic; no guaranteed number of make-up sessions is specified.

## 5. Class delivery changes
The cohort notice identifies whether a course is on campus or online. If a campus becomes unavailable because of an institution-announced closure, Meridian may move the affected session online or announce a replacement date. Students should follow the official cohort notice. This operational change does not itself create a tuition refund entitlement unless the institution cancels the cohort under Section 3.

## 6. Complaints and response process
Students should send a written complaint to students@meridian-university.example with the course name, cohort, a concise description of the issue, and relevant receipt or notice if applicable. Do not include passwords or sensitive banking credentials. The help desk aims to acknowledge a complaint within 2 working days. This is an acknowledgement target, not a resolution guarantee.

## 7. Policy interpretation
This document is the current policy from 2026-08-01. Use it for present-day admissions, payment, refund, attendance, certificate, and complaint questions. The archived policy is retained only for historical comparisons. If another document appears to conflict with this policy and does not identify a later approved version, explain the conflict rather than silently choosing an unsupported rule.
