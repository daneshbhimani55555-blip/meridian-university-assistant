## 1. Published learning offerings
The following eight offerings are fictional catalogue entries. Fees are course tuition amounts in Pakistani rupees (PKR). The separate application fee and payment deadline are defined in the current policy document, not in this catalogue. A listing is not a promise that a new cohort is open on every date.

1. **AI and Data Foundations** — 12 weeks; PKR 18,000. Introduces data handling, spreadsheet analysis, responsible use of AI tools, and basic machine-learning concepts. Students complete a small data interpretation project. No advanced programming background is required, although basic computer use is expected. Delivery: Karachi campus or online, subject to cohort listing.

2. **Web Development** — 16 weeks; PKR 22,000. Covers HTML, CSS, JavaScript fundamentals, responsive page structure, and a guided website project. Students practice building and testing a responsive website. Delivery: Karachi or online, subject to cohort listing.

3. **Cybersecurity Basics** — 10 weeks; PKR 16,500. Introduces account safety, common security concepts, safe browsing, privacy awareness, and defensive security practices. It is an introductory defensive-awareness course. Delivery: Karachi campus.

4. **Graphic Design Essentials** — 8 weeks; PKR 14,000. Covers visual hierarchy, typography, color principles, layout, and the creation of simple digital design assets. Students complete a portfolio task. Delivery: Karachi or Hyderabad when announced.

5. **English Communication** — 8 weeks; PKR 9,500. Focuses on practical speaking, listening, vocabulary, email structure, and short presentations for education and workplace settings. Delivery: Karachi, Hyderabad weekend cohort, or online when listed.

6. **Office Productivity** — 6 weeks; PKR 7,500. Covers document formatting, spreadsheets, presentations, file organization, and collaborative workplace habits. Practice uses sample data. Delivery: Karachi or online.

7. **Digital Marketing** — 8 weeks; PKR 12,500. Introduces audience research, content planning, search fundamentals, campaign measurement, and ethical digital communication. It does not promise advertising credits or customer leads. Delivery: Karachi or online.

8. **Career Readiness** — 4 weeks; PKR 5,000. Covers CV structure, interview preparation, professional communication, teamwork, and job-search planning. The program provides learning support only; it does not guarantee an internship or job offer. Delivery: online or selected campus workshops.

## 2. Student support services
The student help desk explains published course information and directs applicants to admissions. It cannot disclose another student's information. Enrolled students should use their cohort timetable for exact class dates. Students may contact support about missing course materials; private tutoring is not an automatic entitlement.

## 3. Choosing a course
Applicants should compare outcomes, duration, delivery mode, and tuition. If a cohort location or start date is not published, the assistant must not guess and should refer the applicant to admissions.

## 4. Fees and catalogue limitations
The tuition amounts above are the published fictional catalogue fees for this knowledge-base version. They do not include the separate application fee. The current policy document controls application charges, payment deadlines, refunds, and other administrative rules. If a question combines a course tuition fee with a payment deadline, retrieve both this catalogue and the current policy. Do not use an archived policy to answer a question about today's rules.
